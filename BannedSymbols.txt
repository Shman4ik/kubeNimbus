# The client library's kubeconfig loaders all deserialize through a YamlDotNet
# StaticContext that KubernetesClient.Aot ships precompiled against one exact YamlDotNet
# version. With any other YamlDotNet in the graph they throw TypeLoadException at the
# first kubeconfig read, while the build and the NativeAOT publish stay green. Reading
# kubeconfig through KubeNimbus.Core.KubeconfigReader is what lets YamlDotNet and
# KubernetesClient.Aot be upgraded independently; one call to these puts the coupling back.
