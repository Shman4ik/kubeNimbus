# Competitor matrix — jobs, not features

Cumulative. Rows are **jobs a user opens a Kubernetes client to do**; columns are the
products. Each cell says roughly **how many interactions** the job takes from an open
cluster tab and **what is paywalled**, with the evidence behind it in the linked
research. A feature list compares checkboxes; this compares how far each product is
from the job being done.

**How to read a cell.** An *interaction* is one click, one key chord, or one typed
command submitted (`:pods⏎` is one). Counts start with the cluster already connected
and the app already showing something, and they are for the common path, not the
fastest trick. "Not found" means the evidence searched did not show it — it is weaker
than "no", and the cell says which. "Not checked" means nobody looked. Paywalls are
stated per cell where they apply.

Each survey updates cells in place and adds a line to the change log at the bottom; a
cell whose evidence is older than the last survey that touched its row says so.

**kubeNimbus's column is taken from `CHANGELOG.md`, `docs/engineering/` and `src/`**, not
from memory; where an open backlog issue would change a cell, its number is named.

**Radar** (Skyhook, Apache-2.0, a Go binary serving a local web UI, 4.1k stars on
2026-11-14) joined the columns on 2026-11-14 as the open-source newcomer with the most
traction this year. Its cells come from its repository description and two secondary
reviews only, so most say "Not checked".

## Change log

- **2026-09-22 (v0.4.0)** — seeded from `docs/research/` (August 2026) and updated with
  the delta since 2026-08-15: Aptakube 1.19.4–1.20.5, Headlamp v0.45.0, Lens 2026.8/2026.9,
  KubeUI v1.1.0–v1.2.0; FreeLens and k9s have not released since July and June. Two rows
  are new with this survey's deep theme: *rollout history / rollback* and *what changed
  recently*. Rows other than triage, change history, logs, GitOps, palette and install
  carry August evidence that was not re-checked.
- **2026-11-14 (after 1.1.0)** — the kubeNimbus column brought up to 1.1.0 from
  `CHANGELOG.md` 0.5.0–1.1.0 (Applications mode, Unhealthy only, multi-select and bulk
  actions, Service port-forward and the forward list, capacity strip, who am I, live and
  fleet Argo CD, open/new YAML, exec in your own terminal, debug containers, JSON lines and
  Clear in logs). Competitor delta since 2026-09-22: Lens 2026.9.1–2026.11.1 and its current
  tiers, FreeLens v2.0.0, Aptakube 1.21.1–1.22.3 and its new free tier, Headlamp v0.46.0,
  KubeUI v1.3.0–v1.8.1 and the v1.9.0 betas; k9s has still not released since June. Radar
  added as a column. Three rows are new: *edit a Secret's values*, *guard against acting
  on the wrong cluster*, *talk to the cluster through an AI agent*. Rows "find why one pod
  is broken" and "browse CRDs" for Lens and Headlamp carry August evidence.
