# Logs: what people actually ask a Kubernetes desktop client for

*2026-08-17. Question asked: `docs/BACKLOG.md`'s Ready table carries **FEAT-3** — "Tail logs
across every pod of a Deployment/selector in one pane, colour-keyed by pod", P1, size L — and
its Signal column is explicitly a hypothesis. Validate it. Is multi-pod tailing the top log
complaint, or is something cheaper ahead of it?*

**The short version: the FEAT-3 hypothesis is broadly right and one third wrong, and it is not
first.**

- **Right:** multi-pod / workload-level logs is real, cross-tool, upvoted demand (~140 reactions
  across five trackers), and Aptakube does market it prominently — it is the *second* bullet on
  its README and has a landing page of its own.
- **Wrong:** the krew claim. `stern` is a standalone binary, not primarily a krew plugin, and no
  install-count ranking could be reached from here ([stats.krew.dev is egress-blocked](#what-was-searched-and-what-could-not-be-reached)).
  The verifiable half of that sentence is that **stern's own README leads with exactly this
  feature** — which is a better citation anyway.
- **Not first:** **structured/JSON log rendering** outranks it. k9s
  [#364](https://github.com/derailed/k9s/issues/364) alone carries **147 👍** — more than every
  multi-pod issue in the field combined — and the same request appears in Lens, Aptakube,
  Headlamp and k9s again. kubeNimbus has nothing for it.
- And **three separate S-sized gaps** sit under both of them with evidence of their own, of which
  the sharpest is that **kubeNimbus fetches `tailLines: 200` and offers no way to ask for more** —
  a smaller window than any competitor's default, against ~162 reactions of exactly that complaint
  in k9s.

FEAT-3 should stay. It should probably not stay at the top of an L-sized queue while four S-sized,
better-evidenced rows are unfiled.

Two things this table settles before any ranking.

**The GUI field is split down the middle on multi-pod logs**, and the split is by age: the two
products built since 2022 (Aptakube, Headlamp) ship it; the Lens lineage does not and has not for
six years; k9s closed it twice as not planned. It is table stakes for a *new* client and a
long-standing hole in the incumbent — which is exactly the shape of thing worth building.

**kubeNimbus's time window is the smallest in the table.** `tailLines: 200` with no control is
below k9s's default (100 lines but `sinceSeconds` configurable), far below stern's (`--since 48h`,
`--tail -1`), and has no equivalent of Aptakube's "Time Since" at all. `LogBufferLines` (4000) caps
what is *kept*; it does not change what is *fetched*. So today there is no way to see anything
older than the last 200 lines of a container, ever, by any gesture in the app.

| Aptakube | [#348 Be able to parse json log and show it a nice way](https://github.com/aptakube/aptakube/issues/348) | 1 | closed |
| Headlamp | [#4700](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/headlamp/issues/4700), [#7138](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/headlamp/issues/7138) | — | **shipped, being fixed** |

~212 reactions across four projects, and the single largest number in this entire report. Lens
#4320 is the **highest-reacted unshipped item** in the whole `area/logs` label once you discount
line-wrapping (which kubeNimbus already has — see §*Already satisfied* below).

There is a genuine design fork in the evidence, and it should not be pre-decided:

- **Colourise/highlight in place** — Aptakube #337's actual shipped answer, and k9s #1778's ask.
- **Prettify (re-indent the JSON)** — Headlamp's answer, and it is the one causing them ongoing
  trouble: [#7138](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/headlamp/issues/7138) reports prettify
  *losing lines*, and [#4700](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/headlamp/issues/4700) replaced a
  regex JSON detector with brace-bounded parsing after test logs showed counts jumping
  "0, 8, 11, 14" instead of incrementing.
- **Field extraction** — [Aptakube #432](https://github.com/aptakube/aptakube/issues/432) (13
  comments) asks for JSON-pointer field selection with sensible defaults, quoting `.NET`'s
  `LogLevel,Category,Message,Exception` and Java's `level,logger,message,exception`, and describes
  it as what people already do by piping `kubectl logs` through `jq`.

**Fit here is unusually good.** Parsing is `System.Text.Json` `JsonDocument`, which is already this
repo's house style for exactly this class of problem (watch frames, discovery, `DynamicResource`) —
AOT-safe, no source-generated model, no new package. It also *upgrades the existing severity
heuristic for free*: `LogLineViewModel.DetectSeverity` currently token-scans the raw text, which is
why `"level":"warn"` had to be special-cased in its own doc-comment; a parsed `level` field is the
real answer. **Do not reach for a reflection-based structured-logging or log-parsing library** —
that is the class of dependency NativeAOT makes expensive here and cheap in Electron.

**162 reactions in k9s alone**, across four issues that are all the same complaint wearing four
titles, and every one of them closed by a stale bot rather than answered. #1228's reporter puts it
plainly: *"if logs can be pulled from command line, let k9s also show it."* The cause is k9s's own
`logger.tail: 100` / `logger.buffer: 1000` defaults, which are documented in its README — and which
are *configurable*, so k9s users at least have a file to edit.

kubeNimbus users have nothing: `tailLines: 200` is a literal in `StartLogs`, and `tailLines: 1000`
another in `LoadPreviousLogs`. This is the largest gap-to-cost ratio in the report.

Marketing corroboration on the same point: Aptakube ships a **"Time Since"** dropdown whose "Live
Tail" setting means *only new logs*, i.e. the reverse default from ours (search snippet of
`aptakube.com` docs, domain blocked); stern's `--since` defaults to **48h** and `--tail` to −1.

`StreamPodLogsAsync` already takes `tailLines`; `sinceSeconds`/`sinceTime` are one more query
parameter each on the same request builder.

**Non-goal check: this is explicitly not a log store.** It asks the API server for more of what the
kubelet still holds on disk. Nothing is persisted; the existing bounded `LogBufferLines` still caps
what the app keeps. It is the *opposite* of the refused "keep history locally" direction.

~150 reactions across **eleven issues in five repos**, which is the widest spread in this report
even though the peak is lower than JSON's. The duplicate rate is itself a signal: FreeLens has
received the same request five separate times in fifteen months.

Lens #272's body cites stern by name and asks for its container include/exclude semantics. FreeLens
#1460 states the on-call reason directly: *"its really helpful if you dont know on which pod your
request ended up on to search for logs."*

**Marketing emphasis is unambiguous and it is Aptakube's.** "Aggregated Log Viewer" is the second
bullet on [its README](https://github.com/aptakube/aptakube), between "Connect to one or more
clusters simultaneously" and "Resource Diff"; it has a dedicated landing page at
`aptakube.com/aggregated-logs` headed *"Visualize logs from multiple pods in a single view"* and a
marketing blog post *"How to view logs from multiple pods in Kubernetes"* (both domain-blocked here,
cited from search snippets). Its docs describe aggregating over "all pods owned by the resource"
with tabs on Pods, Deployments, ReplicaSets, DaemonSets, StatefulSets **and Services** (via
selector), with pod and container names colourised. Nothing else in the field markets logs at all:
Headlamp's landing is the plugin story, KubeUI gives it one line, k9s gives it a keybinding row.

**Four implementation facts the evidence hands over free**, worth carrying into FEAT-3's design pass:

1. **FreeLens is implementing it right now**, in the open. PR
   [#2418](https://github.com/freelensapp/freelens/pull/2418) (opened 2026-08-15, two days before
   this report) does a **k-way merge on RFC3339 timestamps** with **per-pod colour**, parallel
   fetch that keeps existing logs when one pod goes away, a pod-count badge, and a download named
   after the parent workload. kubeNimbus already requests `timestamps=true` on every stream, so the
   merge key exists today.
2. **Do not select every pod by default.** Aptakube shipped that and then had to change it —
   [#227 "Do not select all pods by default when accessing logs"](https://github.com/aptakube/aptakube/issues/227).
3. **Follow + scroll is the part that breaks.** Headlamp's multi-pod follow has its own bug trail:
   [#3043 "Multi-pod logs slider dont work as expected"](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/headlamp/issues/3043)
   and [#3275 "logs: multi-pod: Fix Follow mode slider position tracking"](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/headlamp/issues/3275)
   (27 comments).
4. **N pods is N long-lived HTTP streams.** stern needed `--max-log-requests` for this. Hard rule 2's
   cancellation discipline is load-bearing here in a way it is not for one pod, and `LogBufferLines`
   would have to become a per-*pane* cap rather than a per-pod one.

**Core has no `labelSelector`.** `ListResourceOnceAsync` takes a `fieldSelector` only
([`ClusterClient.Dynamic.cs`](../../src/KubeNimbus.Core/ClusterClient.Dynamic.cs)); selecting a
workload's pods needs one more query parameter, and following a *rolling* deploy needs a watch on
that selector so new pods join the pane. Both are small; the merge, the colour keying, the pane and
the follow model are the L.

### 4. Search versus filter — a distinction the market made deliberately

kubeNimbus's log box filters: non-matching lines are hidden. Aptakube's maintainer opened
[#32 "Add an option to 'Search' in logs, rather than 'Filter' it"](https://github.com/aptakube/aptakube/issues/32)
**as a separate feature from the filter it already had** — 15 👍, the **top-reacted issue in its
entire `area: logs` label**, closed as shipped. The reasoning is in the body: filtering
*"can be useful to reduce the noise on the stream. But in some cases it's also useful to have a
search functionality"* — highlight the matches, keep the surrounding lines visible, like a browser
or an editor.

| Repo | Issue | Reactions | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aptakube | [#32 Search rather than Filter](https://github.com/aptakube/aptakube/issues/32) | **15 👍**, 6 comments | closed — shipped |
| k9s | [#73 Search/highlight text in view/describe/logs](https://github.com/derailed/k9s/issues/73) | 9 👍 | closed |
| Aptakube | [#351 Log search highlight](https://github.com/aptakube/aptakube/issues/351) | 7 👍 | closed — shipped |
| Aptakube | [#331 Regex in filter](https://github.com/aptakube/aptakube/issues/331) | 4 👍 | **open** |
| Lens | [#6024 Show only filtered logs](https://github.com/lensapp/lens/issues/6024) | 3 👍 | open — asks for *our* behaviour |
| FreeLens | [#1672 Prefill log search with selected text on Ctrl+F](https://github.com/freelensapp/freelens/issues/1672) | 2 | closed |

Lens #6024 is the useful counterweight: someone asking Lens for filter-only behaviour, which is
what kubeNimbus has. So this is "both, switchable", not "we picked wrong".

**The cost here is not the matching, it is the rendering.** Highlighting a run *inside* a line means
building `Inlines` in code or a small custom control — the same argument as
[`Controls/Sparkline.cs`](../../src/KubeNimbus.App/Controls/Sparkline.cs), and the same reason a
charting package was rejected. A bound `SelectableTextBlock` cannot do it. Budget for that, not for
the string search.

Regex, if it comes with this: `System.Text.RegularExpressions` is AOT-safe, but `[GeneratedRegex]`
needs a compile-time pattern, so a **user-typed** pattern must use the interpreted engine —
and `RegexOptions.NonBacktracking` is the right default there, because a user-supplied pattern over
a 4000-line buffer is a catastrophic-backtracking hazard on the UI thread.

### 5. Log stream resilience — reconnect, and surviving a rollout

| Repo | Issue | Signal | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headlamp | [#6256 backend/frontend: Add network-resilient logs and terminal sessions](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/headlamp/issues/6256) | **38 comments** | open |
| Lens | [#8163 Unable to access pod logs following redeployment while Lens is running](https://github.com/lensapp/lens/issues/8163) | open bug, Jan 2025 | open |
| Lens | [#3576 Keep container logs after termination](https://github.com/lensapp/lens/issues/3576) | 3 👍, labelled `p2` | open |
| Aptakube | [#294 Automatically reconnect to log stream when disconnected](https://github.com/aptakube/aptakube/issues/294) | 1 👍 | open |
| Aptakube | [#268 Don't show error when viewing logs tab while containers aren't running yet](https://github.com/aptakube/aptakube/issues/268) | 2 👍 | open |

Reaction counts are low; **comment counts and severity are not**. Lens #8163 is the sharpest: after
a redeploy while Lens is running, logs become unreachable until Lens restarts — *shell still works,
only logs fail*, and `kubectl logs` works fine. That is the exact failure mode a GUI is supposed to
save you from.

Headlamp's fix is directly transferable and is described in its PR: always request timestamps,
reconnect with **`sinceTime` set to the last received line's timestamp**, bound the retries, and
filter duplicates across the seam. kubeNimbus already does the first of those on every stream.

**This one is also an internal-consistency argument, not only a market one.** Hard rule 2 says
"watch connections auto-reconnect with resourceVersion resume + relist on 410 Gone; connection loss
is surfaced in the UI, never a silent hang." Log follow gets the *surfacing* half — `EndLogStreamAsync`
states the reason — and none of the *resume* half: a dropped stream simply ends. The rule's spirit
already covers this; the code does not.

### 6. Multi-*container* in one view — cheaper than multi-pod, and possibly what #827 meant

k9s [#827](https://github.com/derailed/k9s/issues/827) is the second-highest multi-log number in the
field at **62 total / 55 👍** — and its body is genuinely ambiguous, saying "multiple replicas
(meaning multiple containers)". It asks to select containers with space and press `L` for a unified
view with per-container prefixes. It could be read either way and this report will not pretend
otherwise. Closed `not_planned`.

Unambiguous evidence for the multi-container reading:
[Aptakube #422 "improve log viewing for pods with multiple containers"](https://github.com/aptakube/aptakube/issues/422)
(open); Aptakube's viewer selects **pods *and* containers** independently; stern tails all
containers of a pod by default (`--container` defaults to `.*`) and its README's first sentence
names both axes — *"multiple pods … and multiple containers within the pod."*

kubeNimbus's container strip is single-select. A sidecar-plus-app pod — a service mesh, a log
shipper, the `access-log-tailer` in this repo's own sandbox manifests — cannot be read as one stream
today. This is a strictly smaller version of FEAT-3 (no selector resolution, no pod watch, no
cluster-qualified keys, the same k-way merge on timestamps we already have) and would prove the
merge machinery before the L-sized version depends on it.

### 7. Severity filter (not just severity colour)

Headlamp merged [#5338 "log-viewer: Add log severity filter dropdown"](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/headlamp/issues/5338)
into **v0.42.0** (May 2026): a multi-select of ERROR/WARN/INFO/DEBUG, client-side, persisted, and —
the detail worth stealing — **lines with no detectable severity are always shown**, so a noisy-stream
filter cannot hide unstructured output.

**This is marketing/parity, not demand.** No upvoted user issue was found asking for it; it is a
maintainer-authored PR. It is listed anyway because kubeNimbus already computes `LogSeverity` per
line, so the filter is a predicate over data the app has already parsed, and because the "always
show unclassified" rule is precisely the mistake the
[`LogSeverityToBrushConverter` null-brush bug](../../CLAUDE.md) taught this repo once already.

  8 comments, **open**. Headlamp [#1061](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/headlamp/issues/1061)

- **Local timezone for timestamps.** k9s [#1302](https://github.com/derailed/k9s/issues/1302) —

  the server's raw RFC3339 UTC prefix verbatim.
- **`kubectl.kubernetes.io/default-container`.** Lens

  honoured this since 1.21 and stern honours it; kubeNimbus picks "the first app container", which is

  opens on the wrong container.

  *stream-source* toggle. kubeNimbus's `ShowLogTimestamps`/`WrapLogLines` are per-tab and reset for
  every pod opened.

## No evidence found

Stated because these were looked for and the absence is the useful answer:

- **Nobody asks a desktop client to store logs.** Every "I can't see enough" request in this report
  — k9s #1228/#1507/#994/#901, Lens #2172/#1729 — asks for *more of what the API server still has*,
  via tail/since. No analogue was found of the metrics-history request the
  [node-ops report](2026-08-16-node-ops-and-overview.md) turned up
  ([freelens#2409](https://github.com/freelensapp/freelens/issues/2409)). **Nothing proposed below
  needs a local log store**, and the non-goal is not under pressure from this evidence.
- **No evidence found** that "previous container logs" is hard to *find* in any competitor. FreeLens
  #2095/#2096 are the opposite complaint — it was made *too* prominent by being persisted. So the
  brief's "previous-container discoverability" candidate has no support; kubeNimbus's toolbar toggle
  appears to be the right shape already.
- **No evidence found** for log alerting or notification in a desktop client.
- **Very weak evidence** for backend log-store integration (Loki/Elasticsearch/`hl`): only
  [Aptakube #379 "Integrate hl into the logs view"](https://github.com/aptakube/aptakube/issues/379),
  1 reaction. Not proposed.
- **CLI-shaped demand exists and is best answered by a shipped feature, not a new one.** k9s
  [#785 "Piping logs with `|`"](https://github.com/derailed/k9s/issues/785) has **49 👍** and
  [#1229 "Pipe all logs to pager"](https://github.com/derailed/k9s/issues/1229) 8 more. The honest
  kubeNimbus answer is FEAT-16's terminal launcher plus the existing download — not a pipe. Recorded,
  not proposed.
- **Logs from a Service** (Aptakube resolves the selector and shows them) — no user issue found
  asking for it, in any tracker. Mentioned only as a scope note on FEAT-3.

## Verdict on FEAT-3

**Keep it.** It is real demand, it is the widest-spread request in the field, it is table stakes for
a client built after 2022, and it is the one log feature a direct competitor markets. Nothing here
argues for retiring it.

**Two amendments, neither of which touches its scope**, proposed as a row below rather than as an
edit to its text:

1. The Signal's krew claim is **unverified and probably wrong in form**. stern is a standalone
   binary; `stats.krew.dev` was unreachable from here and no published install ranking with numbers
   was found anywhere. Replace it with what is checkable: stern's own README's first sentence, Lens
   #272 (38 👍, open six years, epic), FreeLens's five duplicates plus an in-flight PR, and
   Aptakube's README bullet #2.
2. Its *position* deserves re-examination against §2, §4 and §5, which are S-sized, individually
   evidenced, and each of which improves the single-pod pane that FEAT-3's pane would inherit. In
   particular a merged multi-pod pane still fetching `tailLines: 200` per pod would ship the §2 gap
   multiplied by the replica count.

That is a priority decision and therefore a human's. The rows below are filed with the priority
column blank.
